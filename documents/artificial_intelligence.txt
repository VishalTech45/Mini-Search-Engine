Artificial intelligence (AI) is the computational science of synthesizing intelligent agents capable of perceiving environments, reasoning, learning, and taking optimal actions to achieve goals.
Classical symbolic artificial intelligence utilizes expert systems, knowledge representation, rule-based inference engines, and ontologies.
Heuristic search algorithms solve complex combinatorial state spaces. The A* search algorithm finds optimal shortest paths using admissible heuristic evaluation functions.
Adversarial search algorithms, including minimax with alpha-beta pruning, drive decision-making in competitive games such as chess, checkers, and Go.
Constraint satisfaction problem (CSP) solvers apply backtracking search, forward checking, and arc consistency algorithms to solve scheduling and allocation challenges.
Natural language processing (NLP) enables machines to comprehend, analyze, and generate human language through transformer models, token embeddings, and attention mechanisms.
Knowledge graphs structure semantic relations between real-world entities, enabling automated logical reasoning, question answering, and semantic search engines.
Probabilistic reasoning under uncertainty relies on Bayesian networks, hidden Markov models (HMM), and Markov decision processes (MDP).
Computer vision integrates convolutional filters, object detection models (YOLO), and image segmentation to extract semantic meaning from visual sensor inputs.
Ethical AI initiatives address algorithmic bias, fairness, interpretability, safety alignment, and responsible deployment in automated decision-making systems.
